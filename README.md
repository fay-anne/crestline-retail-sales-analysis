An end-to-end retail sales analysis covering data cleaning, profitability analysis, and interactive dashboard development using Microsoft Excel.

## Project Overview

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, PivotTables, PivotCharts, formulas, slicers, and Timeline.

**Dataset:** Synthetic retail sales transactions (January–December 2025).

**Objective:** Analyse sales performance across products, categories, stores, and time periods to identify revenue drivers, profitability patterns, and business improvement opportunities.

## Data Cleaning & Preparation

- Assessed 8,534 original sales records.
- Removed 34 duplicate Order ID occurrences, retaining 8,500 records.
- Investigated missing values and inconsistent categorical labels.
- Standardised product categories, store names, and payment methods.
- Flagged invalid quantities, prices, costs, and discount rates.
- Calculated Net Sales, COGS, Gross Profit, and Gross Profit Margin.
- Documented data-quality issues and cleaning decisions.

## Key Findings

- **Net Sales:** ₦1.58B
- **Gross Profit:** ₦355.65M
- **Gross Profit Margin:** 22.53%
- **Quantity Sold:** 21,496 units
- **Top Revenue Category:** Electronics
- **Highest Revenue Store:** Port Harcourt
- **Highest Gross Profit Store:** Ikeja
- **Strongest Sales Month:** April

## Dashboard Features

- Four dynamic KPI cards
- Six performance visualisations
- Category and Store slicers
- Interactive date Timeline
- Dynamic trend sparklines and profit margin progress bar

## Business Recommendations

1. Review pricing and costs for low-margin products.
2. Investigate profitability differences across stores.
3. Use product demand trends to support inventory planning.
4. Examine monthly sales patterns to improve business planning.
